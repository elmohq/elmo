How Much Does It Cost to Board a Dog?

By Renee Okafor, operations lead · Updated 2 September 2026

Quick answer

In our 2026 survey of 410 US boarding facilities, the median price for an overnight kennel stay was $48 per night; private suites had a median of $72. Prices in the 10 largest metros were about 30% higher than in smaller cities.

What affects the price

Dog size, length of stay, holiday surcharges, and add-ons such as daily walks or medication administration. Most facilities we surveyed charged a holiday surcharge of $5–$15 per night.

Methodology

We contacted 410 facilities in 38 states between March and May 2026 and recorded posted or quoted prices for a 40 lb dog. Download the data.
