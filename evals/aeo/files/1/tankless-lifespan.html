Our Thoughts

So, how long do they actually last? In our experience servicing units across the metro, a well-maintained tankless unit usually keeps going for around 20 years, which is noticeably longer than the 8–12 years we typically see from tank-style heaters. The biggest factor we see shortening that lifespan is hard water: Twin Cities water is hard, and scale builds up inside the heat exchanger if the unit is never flushed. We recommend descaling (flushing) once a year, or more often if you do not have a water softener. Error codes about flame failure or overheating are often the first sign of scale problems.

Tankless units also save energy because they only heat water on demand. They are great for families of all sizes!
