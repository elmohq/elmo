**Jev (TypeSafe) is now a required dependency of Elmo: the worker won't start without `TYPESAFE_API_KEY`.** Jev judges mentions and sentiment, which is far more accurate toward your brand and each competitor. It costs very roughly $0.15–0.35 per 1,000 answers that name a tracked company, more for long answers that name many. Upgrading re-judges the last 90 days of answers once, about three months' worth of that cost; set `REPROCESS_DAYS=all` before upgrading to re-judge your whole history instead.
