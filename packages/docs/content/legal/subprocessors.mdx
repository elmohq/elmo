We use third-party subprocessors — cloud providers, payment and email services, and the AI and search platforms Elmo queries — to run Elmo Cloud. Each is engaged under data protection terms, in most cases the vendor's standard data processing addendum, extending the safeguards described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) everywhere personal data is processed.

This page lists them. It applies to **Elmo Cloud only**. If you self-host Elmo, you choose your own infrastructure and providers, and none of this applies to you.

## AI and search data providers

This is the part that makes Elmo work: to measure how answer engines describe a brand, Elmo has to ask them. Prompts you configure are sent to these providers, either directly through their APIs or through a scraping provider that queries the consumer product on our behalf. The answers come back to us and are stored in your account.

**What they receive is your prompt text and the domains you track** — market-research queries like "best project management software for agencies". They do not receive your name, your email address, your billing details, or anything else about your account. Do not put personal data into a prompt.

Which of these is used depends on the platforms your plan tracks and how Elmo Cloud is configured at the time; we update this list when that changes.

## Getting notified when this changes

This page lives in the [Elmo repository](https://github.com/elmohq/elmo/tree/main/packages/docs/content/legal). Watch that folder on GitHub and you'll see every change to this list, with a diff, before we announce it anywhere else.

If you have a signed agreement with us that requires advance notice of new subprocessors, we'll email you as well. To object to a new subprocessor, email [privacy@elmohq.com](mailto:privacy@elmohq.com).
