The privacy of your data — and it is your data, not ours — is a big deal to us. This policy lays out what we collect and why, how it's handled, and what rights you have over it. We have never sold personal data and we never will.

This policy applies to **Blue Whale Software, LLC** ("we", "our", "us") and to the hosted service at [app.elmohq.com](https://app.elmohq.com), our websites at elmohq.com and docs.elmohq.com, and our APIs (together, the "Services").

**It does not apply to self-hosted Elmo.** If you run the open-source project yourself, your instance sends us nothing beyond optional, anonymous version telemetry that you can turn off, and we never see the data inside it. See [Telemetry](/docs/developer-guide/telemetry) for exactly what that is.

This policy covers our handling of information about site visitors, prospective customers, and customers and their authorized users. We refer to all of you as "you" throughout.

It does **not** cover information about a customer's own end users that we process on that customer's behalf. For that data the customer is the controller and we are the processor; our obligations are set out in the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and any data processing addendum we've signed with them.

## What we collect and why

Our guiding principle is to collect only what we need.

### Identity and access

When you sign up we ask for your name, email address, and a password (or you sign in with Google, in which case we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier). That's so you can have an account, so we can send you the things you need, and so your teammates can tell who's who.

We use your email address for service notifications: verification, password resets, invitations, billing notices, and important product changes. With your consent we'll also send you product updates. You can unsubscribe from those at any time without losing the ones you need.

We will never sell your personal information, and we won't use your name or your company in marketing without your permission.

### Billing information

If you subscribe to a paid plan you'll provide payment details and a billing address. Card details are submitted directly to our payment processor, Stripe, and never touch Elmo's servers. We store a record of the transaction — amount, date, and the last four digits of the card — for account history, invoicing, and billing support. We store your billing address so we can charge you correctly, calculate tax, and detect fraud.

### Product content

We store the content you create in Elmo: the brands you track, the competitors and prompts you configure, the answers Elmo collects from AI answer engines on your behalf, and the reports built from them. This is what the product is. We keep it as long as your account is active, and delete it after cancellation as described below.

### General geolocation data

We log the IP address used to create an account and retain it to mitigate spam signups. We log account sign-ins by IP for security and fraud prevention, and keep that login data for as long as the account is active.

### Website interactions

We collect information about browsing activity on our websites for analytics and statistical purposes — which pages were visited, how long they took to load, what referred you, and general browser and device information.

We use two analytics tools, and they behave differently:

- **[Plausible](https://plausible.io/privacy-focused-web-analytics)** is our primary web analytics. It sets **no cookies**, stores nothing on your device, and does not collect or store personal data or cross-site identifiers. We proxy it through our own domain so it isn't blocked by ad blockers, not to collect anything extra.
- **[PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy)** is our product analytics. It **does** set cookies and store an identifier in your browser, so on our marketing site we only load it where we're allowed to — see [Advertising and cookies](#advertising-and-cookies) below. Inside the product it's tied to your account and used to understand which features get used.

We do not record your screen or your session.

### Advertising and cookies

We advertise Elmo on third-party platforms, and where permissible under law we load advertising and conversion-measurement tags from those platforms — currently or prospectively including **Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn, X, and Reddit**. These tags set third-party cookies and report back which ad you clicked and whether you did something afterwards, such as starting a signup. They are how we tell whether our advertising works at all.

We also use first-party cookies to keep you signed in, to remember preferences, and to remember your cookie choices.

**Where consent is required — in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland — no analytics or advertising cookie is set until you accept one.** You'll see a banner on your first visit and you can change your answer at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link in our footer. Strictly necessary cookies, such as your session and your consent record itself, are always set; without them the site cannot work.

The full list of what we set and why is in our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).

You can also adjust cookie retention and block individual cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the product will not work if you turn cookies off entirely.

### Support and correspondence

When you email us, or start a chat with us on our website, we keep that correspondence — including your email address — so we have a history to reference if you get in touch again. Chat is provided by a third party listed in our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

We also store information you volunteer, like responses to a survey or a research call. If we want to record a conversation, we'll ask first.

### Error monitoring

We use Sentry to collect diagnostic information when something breaks — a stack trace, the browser and version, the URL, and the account identifier associated with the request. This is how we find and fix bugs. We do not send prompt content or answer text to it.

## When we access or disclose your information

**To provide the Services.** We use third-party subprocessors for hosting, storage, email, payments, error monitoring, analytics, and the AI and search providers Elmo queries on your behalf. The current list, what each one does, and where it operates, is published at [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors). We put data processing agreements in place with each of them.

**With your direction.** If you connect a third-party service to Elmo, or ask us to send data somewhere, we'll do that.

**To help you, with your permission.** If we need to look at your account to resolve a support case, we'll ask you first.

**To investigate or act on prohibited use.** Accessing an account while investigating abuse is a last resort. See our [Acceptable Use policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

**Aggregated and de-identified data.** We may aggregate or de-identify information collected through the Services and use it for any purpose, including improving Elmo and publishing industry research. This never includes anything that identifies you, your organization, or your brands.

**When required under applicable law.** We are a U.S. company and our data infrastructure is located in the United States.

- *Requests for user data.* We do not respond to government requests for user data unless compelled by legal process, or in a genuine emergency. If U.S. law enforcement has the necessary warrant, subpoena, or court order, we must comply. We will only respond to non-U.S. authorities if compelled through a mutual legal assistance treaty. Our policy is to notify affected users before disclosing data unless we are legally prohibited from doing so.
- *Preservation requests.* We comply with preservation requests only when legally compelled, and destroy preserved copies at the end of the preservation period unless a proper order arrives first.
- *Tax audits.* If a tax authority audits us we may have to disclose billing information. We would disclose the minimum needed.

**If we're acquired.** We don't plan on it, but if Blue Whale Software is acquired by or merges with another company, we'll notify you well before any personal information is transferred or becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## Your rights over your information

We apply the same data rights to every customer, wherever you are:

- **Right to know.** What personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold. This policy is that disclosure.
- **Right of access.** To the personal information we hold about you, and to information about how it is shared, stored, secured, and processed.
- **Right to correction.** To have inaccurate personal information corrected.
- **Right to erasure.** To have your personal information erased, subject to limits under applicable law. Fulfilling some deletion requests means we can no longer provide the Services, so a deletion request may result in closing your account.
- **Right to restrict processing** and **right to object**, in the situations where applicable law provides them.
- **Right to portability.** To receive your data and move it elsewhere. You can export your data from the product at any time, and our API gives you programmatic access to everything in your account.
- **Right not to be subject to automated decision-making** that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you. We don't do this.
- **Right to non-discrimination.** We will not charge you differently, or give you worse service, because you exercised a privacy right.
- **Right to complain** to your supervisory authority.

Many of these you can exercise yourself by signing in and changing your account. For anything else, email [privacy@elmohq.com](mailto:privacy@elmohq.com). We may need to verify your identity before responding — usually by confirming your name and the email address on the account. If an authorized agent contacts us on your behalf, we'll need written consent from the account holder first.

If we deny a request and applicable law gives you a right to appeal, we'll explain how in our response.

## How we secure your data

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Our database backups are encrypted. Third-party credentials you store in Elmo are encrypted at rest with a key held outside the database.

Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication, and logged.

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email [security@elmohq.com](mailto:security@elmohq.com) rather than filing a public issue. Our full disclosure policy is in the [repository](https://github.com/elmohq/elmo/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## What happens when you delete something

Content you delete inside the product is removed from the application immediately. Copies may persist in our backups for up to 30 days, after which they are purged.

If you cancel your account, your content becomes inaccessible immediately, is deleted from our active systems within 30 days, and is purged from backups within 60 days. After that we cannot recover it.

## Data retention

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and then we delete or aggregate it. Where this policy states a specific retention period, that period applies. We may keep information longer where we have to — to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute, or enforce our agreements.

## Location of the site and your data

The Services are operated in the United States. **If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or anywhere else outside the United States, any information you provide will be transferred to and stored in the United States.**

## Transfers of personal data out of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Personal data transferred out of the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland must be given equivalent protection. Where we act as a processor for a customer subject to those laws, we will enter into a data processing addendum incorporating the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where relevant). Email [privacy@elmohq.com](mailto:privacy@elmohq.com) to request one.

Occasional transfers that aren't covered by a customer agreement — someone signing up for our newsletter, or filling in a contact form — are made under the [Article 49(1)(b) derogation](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-49-gdpr/).

## Children

The Services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email [privacy@elmohq.com](mailto:privacy@elmohq.com) and we'll delete it.

## Changes and questions

We may update this policy to reflect new practices or new legal requirements. Every revision is tracked publicly in the [Elmo repository](https://github.com/elmohq/elmo/commits/main/packages/docs/content/legal). When we make a significant change we'll refresh the date at the top of this page and notify account holders.

Questions, comments, or concerns about this policy, your data, or your rights? Email [privacy@elmohq.com](mailto:privacy@elmohq.com) and we'll do our best to answer.
