This policy explains what **Blue Whale Software, LLC** stores in your browser when you visit our websites or use Elmo Cloud, and what control you have over it. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

"Cookies" here is shorthand. It covers cookies, `localStorage`, and any other technology that stores or reads something on your device.

## The short version

- Our web analytics is cookieless today — [Plausible](https://plausible.io/privacy-focused-web-analytics), which sets **no cookies**. Most of what we know about our website traffic comes from it.
- **Where consent is required — currently the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland — we set no analytics or advertising cookie until you accept one.** A banner asks on your first visit; nothing but strictly necessary storage is written before you answer.
- Everywhere else, analytics and advertising cookies are on by default, and you can turn them off from the **Cookie preferences** control on our site.
- Where we run **session replay**, it sits in the analytics category and follows your analytics choice — so in a consent region nothing is recorded until you accept.

## Categories

The tables below describe what we set at the time of writing. The categories are the part that matters — what lands in each one changes as the tools we use change, and your choices apply to the category, not to a particular cookie.

### Strictly necessary

These are always set. The site cannot work without them, and consent is not required for them under the ePrivacy Directive.

| What | Where | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `better-auth.session_token` | app.elmohq.com | Keeps you signed in | Session, up to 30 days |
| CSRF and callback state | app.elmohq.com | Protects sign-in and form submissions from cross-site request forgery | Minutes |
| `elmo.cookie-consent` (localStorage) | elmohq.com, app.elmohq.com | Remembers your answer to the cookie banner so we don't ask again | Until you clear it |
| Load-balancing and security cookies | all | Set by our hosting providers to route requests and mitigate abuse | Session |
| Sentry session identifier (sessionStorage) | app.elmohq.com | Correlates errors from the same visit so a crash report is intelligible. No cookie. | Until the tab closes |

### Third-party embeds

Some pages embed content from other services, which set their own storage when you interact with them:

- **YouTube** — today we embed through `youtube-nocookie.com`, which does not set tracking cookies unless you press play.
- **Mux** — video playback on some product pages.
- **Live chat** — our support chat widget stores a conversation identifier so your chat history survives a page reload.

## Changing your mind

Look for **Cookie preferences** — it's in the footer of our website, and in the legal menu in Elmo Cloud. Your choice takes effect immediately: turning analytics off stops further collection and clears the identifier.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use your browser's Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control setting. We honor Global Privacy Control as an opt-out of advertising cookies. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop you from being able to sign in.
