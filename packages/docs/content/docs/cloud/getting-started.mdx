## 1. Create your account

[Create an Elmo Cloud account](https://app.elmohq.com/auth/register?returnTo=%2Fapp%2Fnew) with email and password or Google. Email signups must verify their address before continuing. Elmo creates your first workspace automatically.

## 2. Choose and pay for a plan

An unpaid workspace is sent to the plan picker. Choose monthly or annual billing and complete the secure Stripe Checkout. Annual billing includes two months free.

Canceling or abandoning Checkout does not create a partial subscription. Return to Elmo and choose a plan again when you are ready.

## 3. Set up your brand

Enter the brand name and primary website. Elmo analyzes the public website and suggests:

- aliases and additional domains;
- competitors and their domains;
- an initial set of prompts.

Review the suggestions before saving. Enabled prompts count against the workspace-wide prompt pool; disabled prompts remain saved without consuming tracking capacity.

## 4. Choose tracking platforms

Starter tracks ChatGPT automatically. During onboarding, Basic, Pro, and Business ask you to choose exactly four standard platforms for the brand.

Pro and Business can also assign onboarding prompts to daily Claude tracking. Each assigned prompt can use the Claude base model or Claude's native web search, drawing from the workspace's separate Claude allowance. After onboarding, use **Settings → LLMs** to review or change platform and Claude assignments.

## 5. Let tracking begin

Saving onboarding activates the brand and its schedules. Results appear as the worker completes the first evaluations; you do not need to trigger each prompt manually.
