---

## Is there a free trial?

No. The [live demo](https://demo.elmohq.com) and the complete [self-hosted edition](/docs/getting-started) are the free evaluation paths. A new Cloud workspace starts tracking after Checkout succeeds.

## What does annual billing cost?

Annual billing charges ten months of the monthly plan price, giving you two months free. Claude add-on capacity follows the same interval: $5 per assigned prompt monthly or $50 per assigned prompt annually.

## How do I change plans?

Workspace owners and admins can open **Plan & usage**, choose monthly or annual billing, and select another self-service plan. Elmo checks current usage before asking Stripe to change the subscription. If a downgrade would exceed a limit, the page lists what must be reduced first.

Custom plans are changed through Elmo support because their limits and target policies come from the contract.

## Where are invoices and payment methods?

Use **Manage payment method and invoices in Stripe** on the Plan & usage page. The signed Stripe Customer Portal session contains invoices, payment methods, and cancellation controls for the workspace's customer record.

## What happens after a failed payment?

Tracking remains available during a seven-day payment grace period. Plan & usage shows the deadline and links to Stripe. If the invoice is still unpaid when the grace period ends, tracking pauses until Stripe reports the subscription as usable again.

## What happens when I cancel?

Tracking remains available through the paid billing period, then pauses. The Plan & usage page shows the effective end date. You can choose a new self-service plan later, or contact support for a custom contract.

## Who can manage billing?

Workspace owners and admins can start Checkout, change plans, adjust Claude capacity, and open the Stripe portal. Members can see the current plan and usage but cannot mutate billing.

## Are taxes included in the listed price?

Listed prices exclude any applicable tax. Stripe calculates tax from the billing details supplied during Checkout.
